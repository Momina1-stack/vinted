How referrals work

Share your link

Invite people to join Vinted by sharing your link with them.

Tell your friends to list

If they list 3 items within 7 days of joining Vinted, you'll get 2 coupons worth 2,50 $ each. If they sell an item within 30 days of joining, you'll get another 5 coupons worth 2,00 $ each.

Spend your coupon

Buy an item that costs 15,00 $ or more (not including shipping), and one of your coupons will be automatically deducted from the item price.
