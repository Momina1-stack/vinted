Feedback & star ratings

On Vinted, feedback helps keep our community safe, fair, and friendly while building trust among members.

To view a member's feedback and star rating, go to their profile and select “Reviews”.

Member feedback

Members can choose star ratings and leave feedback for each other after an order is completed.

It's not possible to leave feedback for members with whom you haven’t traded, and there’s no monetary compensation for leaving feedback on Vinted.

Automatic feedback

Vinted automatic feedback will be added if neither the seller nor the buyer leaves feedback for each other.

  • Sellers will receive positive auto feedback if the buyer doesn’t leave feedback within 4 days after the order is completed
  • Both sellers and buyers will receive negative auto feedback if the order is cancelled manually or the item isn’t shipped on time.
