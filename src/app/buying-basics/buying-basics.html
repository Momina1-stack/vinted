Buying basics

1.Find an item you like

Check your newsfeed to find something you’d like to buy.

  • •
    Check out more tips on finding the best items..
  • •
    When you find a match, favourite that listing to easily find it later.

2. Contact the seller

  • •
    Press “Ask seller” to reach out to the seller. Double-check the item’s dimensions before buying it.
  • •
    Press “Make an offer” to suggest a new price.
  • •
    Look through the seller's reviews for more credibility. See what other members say about their purchases with that seller.
  • •

    If a seller has more listings you’re interested in, you can create a bundle purchase..

Important

Always stay on Vinted when chatting to other members, and don’t share your personal information.. Never move your conversation to another app or SMS.

Here are more tips on chatting safely with other members.

3. Pay for the item

Press “Buy now” to proceed to the checkout. Buyer Protection applies to all purchases made via the “Buy now” button. At the checkout:

  1. Enter your address and phone number (if asked)
  2. Choose the most suitable payment method
  3. Select a delivery option for your order
  4. Finally, press “Pay” to confirm your order
  • The Buyer Protection fee will be added automatically to the final price of your order.
  • If you've selected a verification service, you'll see its cost at the checkout too.

If the “Buy now” button isn’t visible, ask if the seller has enabled all available carriers — it will ensure that a carrier compatible with your location and item category is available to you.

4. Receive your item

  • The seller has 5 business days to send the item (unless you agree to extend the deadline).
  • Delivery usually takes up to 5-7 business days.
    • If delivery is taking longer, don’t worry. You can follow the order status on Vinted and the tracking info and stay in touch with the seller.

5. Let us know if everything’s OK

Once you’re notified by Vinted that the item should have been delivered or that the order seems lost, you’ll see two buttons:

  • “Everything is OK” — pressing this button will release your payment to the seller.
  • “I have an issue” — press this button if a problem arose and submit your claim. Do this within 2 days of receiving a notification from Vinted about the item’s delivery.

After pressing “I have an issue”, try to find a mutual agreement with the seller first. If you need help finding a solution, contact us. We’ll then look into the case and, if applicable, refund you .

Loved your shopping experience with a particular seller? Let them know!

You can rate the seller and leave your feedback to encourage others to buy from them or share your thoughts on what didn’t go smoothly.

Good to know

We recommend washing or disinfecting the items before using them (note that used and washed items are no longer refundable).
