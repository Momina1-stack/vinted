Frequent Uploads badge

By keeping up your listing streak, you’ll encourage buyers to check out more of your items.

How to earn this badge:

  • List 5 items in a 30-day period.
  • Each day, we’ll review your status. If you meet the criteria, buyers will see the badge on your profile and listings.
  • You’ll keep your badge even if you sell an item. If you hide or delete an item, however, you’ll lose your progress.
  • Listed items must comply with our Catalogue Rules and Terms and Conditions .
