Selling basics

Important

For your best experience and safety, be sure to never leave Vinted throughout the trading process and when chatting to other members. It includes everything you need to sell successfully — from tracked and compensated shipping to verified reviews from real buyers, and support if things don't go as expected.

1. List your items for sale

  • •
    See what you can sell on Vinted.
  • •
    Follow our instructions on how to upload an item.
  • •

    There are no fees for selling on Vinted, and you can list as many items as you like.

  • •
    If you don't want to use specific carriers to ship your items, adjust your shipping options.

Note

Once you upload an item, it might get screened automatically to go through a standard check to make sure it follows our Catalog Rules and guidelines for selling. Normally, it takes up to 1 minute but, in some cases, can take up to 1 or 2 days.

2. Increase your chances to sell

  • •
    You can help your items get noticed faster with Item Bumps and Showcase.
  • •

    Check out more tips on how to sell your items faster.

3. Sold! It’s time to ship the item

  • Pack your item securely.
  • Send the item within 5 business days after it’s bought (unless the buyer agrees to extend this deadline). Otherwise the order will be canceled automatically and the buyer will be refunded.
  • Use the shipping option your buyer has chosen to avoid any issues with parcel tracking or even canceled orders.
  • You can track your parcel in the conversation screen with the buyer.
  • If it’s your first sale, don’t forget to activate your Vinted Wallet in order to receive the payment when the order is completed.

4. Delivered! Get your earnings and leave feedback

  • After the buyer gets the item and confirms that everything is OK, the order will be completed.
  • You’ll receive the payment for the order in your Vinted Wallet within 2 days after the order is completed.
  • Money withdrawals may take up to 5 business days to appear in your bank account.
  • Leave feedback to your buyer and help build trust within the community.

Good to know

  • If you close an empty locker compartment and cannot resend the item using the same label, you need to cancel the order. Please explain what happened to the buyer, and ask them to buy that item again via a new label with a new price.
  • To build a trusted profile and reassure your buyers, add your profile picture and verify your account .
  • If you ever experience any improper behavior, harassment or spam activities from other members, we encourage you to report them so we can put a stop to it immediately.
