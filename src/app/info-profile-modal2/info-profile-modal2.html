Speedy Shipping badge

Win over buyers with a badge to show that you'll send their items promptly.

How to earn it:

  • Once you're notified that an item has sold, try to send it within the next 24 hours.
  • Sundays and public holidays are excluded.
  • Each day, we'll review your previous 30 days of activity and update your status. If you meet the criteria, buyers will see the badge on your profile and listings.

Tip: Shipping items faster means you may get paid quicker, too.
